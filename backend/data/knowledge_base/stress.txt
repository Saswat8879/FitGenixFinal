Stress Management

Chronic stress contributes to weight gain, high blood pressure, heart disease, and weakened immunity.

Physical signs of stress:
- Elevated heart rate and blood pressure
- Muscle tension (especially neck, shoulders, jaw)
- Headaches and digestive issues
- Difficulty sleeping

Evidence-based stress reduction techniques:
- Deep breathing (4-7-8 method): inhale 4 sec, hold 7 sec, exhale 8 sec. Do 3-4 cycles.
- Progressive muscle relaxation: tense and release each muscle group for 5-10 seconds
- Mindfulness meditation: even 5-10 minutes daily reduces perceived stress by 25% (JAMA)
- Physical activity: 20-30 minutes of moderate exercise releases endorphins
- Nature exposure: 20 minutes in green spaces lowers cortisol levels

Stress and lifestyle disease:
- Stressed individuals are 50% more likely to develop Type 2 diabetes
- Chronic stress raises LDL cholesterol and blood pressure
- Stress eating leads to caloric surplus, particularly from high-sugar/high-fat foods

Workplace stress management:
- Take a 5-minute break every 60-90 minutes
- Practice the 20-20-20 rule for screen fatigue (every 20 min, look 20 feet away for 20 sec)
- Desk stretches: neck rolls, wrist circles, shoulder shrugs
- Maintain social connections — brief conversations reduce stress hormones

When to seek professional help:
- Persistent anxiety or depressed mood lasting more than 2 weeks
- Inability to perform daily tasks
- Using substances to cope with stress
- Thoughts of self-harm — contact a mental health professional immediately
