Exercise and Physical Activity Guidelines

The World Health Organization recommends for adults (18-64 years):
- At least 150-300 minutes of moderate aerobic activity per week, OR
- 75-150 minutes of vigorous aerobic activity per week
- Muscle-strengthening activities on 2 or more days per week

For people with chronic conditions:
- Start slow — even 5-10 minutes of walking counts
- COPD/Asthma: target 20 minutes daily instead of 30, use a bronchodilator before exercise
- Knee/Back pain: prefer low-impact exercises (swimming, cycling, yoga)
- Hypertension: avoid heavy lifting and isometric exercises; favor aerobic activities
- Obesity: start with walking and gradually increase intensity

Strength training benefits:
- Increases muscle mass and metabolic rate
- Improves insulin sensitivity (beneficial for diabetics)
- Strengthens bones and reduces injury risk
- Recommended: 2-3 sets of 8-12 reps per exercise, 2-3 days per week

Exercise timing matters:
- Morning exercise (6-10 AM) may boost metabolism throughout the day
- Avoid intense exercise within 2 hours of a large meal
- Post-meal walks (10-15 min after eating) can reduce blood sugar spikes by up to 30%

Recovery is essential:
- Rest at least 48 hours between strength sessions targeting the same muscle group
- Sleep 7-9 hours for optimal recovery and muscle growth
- Hydrate before, during, and after exercise
