Weight Management

Sustainable weight management is about long-term habits, not crash diets.

Energy balance fundamentals:
- Weight loss: consume 300-500 fewer calories than you burn daily
- Weight gain/muscle building: consume 200-400 more calories with strength training
- Maintenance: match calorie intake to expenditure
- 1 kg of fat ≈ 7700 calories of energy

Healthy rate of change:
- Weight loss: 0.5-1 kg per week (faster rates risk muscle loss)
- Muscle gain: 0.25-0.5 kg per month for beginners (with proper training)
- Plateaus are normal — adjust calories or exercise after 2-3 weeks of no change

BMI categories (WHO):
- Underweight: <18.5
- Normal: 18.5-24.9
- Overweight: 25-29.9
- Obese Class I: 30-34.9
- Obese Class II: 35-39.9
- Obese Class III: ≥40

Strategies that work (evidence-based):
- Track food intake: people who log meals lose 50% more weight
- Eat protein at every meal (increases satiety)
- High-fiber foods slow digestion and keep you full longer
- Sleep 7+ hours — poor sleep increases hunger hormones (ghrelin)
- Regular weigh-ins (weekly) help maintain awareness
- Don't eliminate entire food groups — sustainable diets allow flexibility

Common pitfalls:
- Skipping meals leads to overeating later
- "Diet" products often replace fat with sugar
- Emotional eating: identify triggers and develop alternative coping strategies
- Excessive restriction leads to binge-restrict cycles
