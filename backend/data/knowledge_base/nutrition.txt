Nutrition and Healthy Eating

Balanced macronutrient targets (general adult):
- Carbohydrates: 45-65% of total calories (prefer complex carbs)
- Protein: 10-35% of total calories (0.8-1.2g per kg body weight)
- Fat: 20-35% of total calories (limit saturated fat to <10%)

Key micronutrients to track:
- Fiber: at least 25g/day (whole grains, fruits, vegetables, legumes)
- Sodium: less than 2300mg/day (1500mg if hypertensive)
- Added sugar: less than 25g/day for women, 36g for men (AHA)
- Potassium: 2600-3400mg/day (bananas, potatoes, spinach)

Meal timing and frequency:
- Eat at regular intervals (3 main meals + 1-2 snacks)
- Keep 3+ hours between major meals for digestion
- Avoid eating large meals after 9 PM (disrupts sleep, promotes fat storage)
- Morning hydration: drink water within 30 minutes of waking

Special dietary considerations:
- Vegetarian: ensure adequate B12, iron, and omega-3s
- Vegan: supplement B12, consider vitamin D and zinc
- Keto: monitor electrolytes, may not be suitable for people with kidney disease
- Diabetic: focus on glycemic index, consistent carb intake, pair carbs with protein/fat

Portion control tips:
- Use smaller plates (9-inch instead of 12-inch)
- Fill half the plate with vegetables
- Measure oils and dressings (1 tbsp = ~120 calories)
- Eat slowly — it takes 20 minutes for satiety signals to reach the brain
